How smart monitoring works

AI does the watching. Humans do the fixing. Nothing is left waiting.

📡

Live data every 15 minutes

Inverter data from Growatt, Sungrow, Deye and PVblink flows into one platform, whatever brand you have.
🧠

AI compares, not guesses

Every evening each system is compared with nearby systems, the day's real sunshine, and its own 30-day normal. Cloudy days are ignored automatically.
🧽

Smart cleaning alerts

If generation stays below normal for 2 sunny days while neighbours are fine, we know the panels need cleaning, before your bill tells you.
⚡

Grid fault detection

Power cut in your area? The system knows it is the grid, not your solar, and waits instead of sending an engineer for nothing.
👷

Human follow-up

Real faults go to our service team. Every 24 hours the system asks for an update until it is resolved. No complaint is forgotten.
✅

Auto-closes when fixed

The moment your inverter is healthy again, the alert closes on its own, with a full history kept.
